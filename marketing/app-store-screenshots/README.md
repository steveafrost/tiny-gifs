# Native App Store screenshot capture requirements

**Status: no final screenshot is approved for App Store Connect upload yet.**

A build-24 iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator capture was made during launch preparation, then deliberately rejected: it was an installer screen rather than the primary product flow and did not meet the desired high-contrast, marketing-quality standard. It is not committed or included in this package.

## Required before App Store Connect upload

1. **Lead screenshot (order 1):** Capture the current expanded Messages drawer with real GIPHY trending or search results. It must show the full, untruncated grid—not the retired 1.5-row viewport.
2. **Supporting screenshot (order 2):** Capture the optional keyboard search/copy flow in a supported chat field after Full Access has been intentionally enabled.
3. Optionally capture the companion keyboard-install flow only after its contrast is visually improved; it should never lead the set.
4. Use raw native iPhone screenshots at a consistent device class, inspect each against the final build, and do not use personal conversation data or composited/cropped product UI.

## Upload gate

These are review requirements only. The owner must approve the final captures and publish them in App Store Connect. The package deliberately does not automate screenshot upload.
